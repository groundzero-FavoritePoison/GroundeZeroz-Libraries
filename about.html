About

The Story Behind GroundeZérö

Writer of dark romance and gothic fiction. Here's the bio, the journey, and the philosophy behind the pen name.

GZ

GroundeZérö writes stories about people who know better and do it anyway — the CEO who shouldn't want her employee, the stranger who shouldn't be trusted, the love interest who is, on paper, a terrible idea.

Before the pen name, there was a science degree and a habit of noticing that formulas explain reactions but never quite explain people. Fiction turned out to be the better lab — the place to actually test what happens when desire meets consequence, and neither one backs down.

Writing from Nigeria, publishing worldwide, and building GroundeZérö one serialized chapter at a time — across GoodNovel, NovelSnack, Amazon KDP, and this library.

The Journey

How the Library Got Built

First Words

Writing for readers, not drafts

Started writing serialized fiction for readers directly — learning pacing, hooks, and cliffhangers one chapter release at a time.

Going Independent

GroundeZérö takes shape

Launched the GroundeZérö pen name and began submitting original dark romance serials to GoodNovel and NovelSnack.

Print & Page

First anthology published

Seven Guilty Pleasures went live on Amazon KDP — four novellas, one shared thread of obsession.

Building the Library

Beyond one pen name

Expanded into ghostwriting for other authors, grew a catalog across platforms and pen names, and started building this site to hold it all in one place.
